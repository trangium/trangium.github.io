Domino Reduction Solver

Finds a shortest move sequence that brings a scrambled cube into G1 = <U, D, R2, L2, F2, B2> — i.e. the shortest solution counting the cube as solved once corners and U/D-face edges have correct orientation only (their permutation doesn't matter) and the four equator edges are in the equator slice.

Or try the reduction game and reduce a scramble one move at a time yourself.


Examples (click to try):
L' U R' U2 → L U' R
R2 U R2 U F2 R2 F B R2 F B → (empty)
R' U R' F' R U R' U' R' → F R2 U' R
