Domino Reduction Game

Reduce the scramble into G1 = <U, D, R2, L2, F2, B2> one move at a time. Enter a move; it's accepted only if it strictly reduces the distance to G1, otherwise it's rejected and you try again. Your score is the product of the number of tries it took at each step — lower is better. Each scramble is a genuine random-state scramble (a uniformly random cube state, solved and inverted), not just random moves. See the solver for background on what G1 means here.
